MAINTENANCE INCIDENT REPORT
Report ID: MNT-2024-002
Date: 2024-02-12
Technician: A. Singh
Priority: Medium

SUMMARY
Routine inspection of Monitor HD units at the South warehouse following a customer complaint about display flickering. Units affected are the 27-inch Monitor HD model, product code MN-5543.

DESCRIPTION
Three units of the Monitor HD 27 (MN-5543) at warehouse WH-SOUTH were found to exhibit intermittent backlight flickering under sustained load. The issue was reproduced in two of the three units under test. The third unit passed all tests.

Customer Elena Petrova (premium tier, West region) submitted the original complaint after receiving her order (ORD-20240210, which was subsequently cancelled before shipment). No units had shipped at the time of this report.

ROOT CAUSE
The backlight driver firmware version 2.1.3 shipped with the current stock batch has a known issue under high-brightness settings. The manufacturer has released firmware 2.1.5 which resolves the problem.

AFFECTED UNITS
- Product: Monitor HD 27 (product code MN-5543, also referred to internally as "the 27-inch unit" or "monitor unit MN-5543")
- Warehouse: WH-SOUTH
- Units affected: 2 of 3 tested; remaining 12 untested units quarantined

ACTIONS TAKEN
1. Firmware update applied to the 2 confirmed affected units — both passed re-test.
2. Remaining 12 units at WH-SOUTH scheduled for firmware update before release.
3. Order ORD-20240210 cancellation confirmed — no units shipped to customer.

NEXT STEPS
Apply firmware 2.1.5 to all Monitor HD 27 (MN-5543) stock across all warehouses as a preventative measure.
